# InstaJam

**Hum it → write it → make it → mix it → ship it.**

A phone-first, installable web app (PWA) that turns a hummed idea into a song:

- **Hum** — tap the big button and sing, hum, whistle or beatbox your idea. Hums stay on your phone.
- **Write** — six AI seats (lyricist, composer, arranger, producer, the critic, prompt smith) shape it into a song and a ready prompt for any music generator.
- **Make** — take the prompt to your generator of choice.
- **Mix** — two turntables, crossfader, EQ, remix pads, a jukebox for your own songs, and a mix recorder.
- **Ship / Film** — SONOID and REELOID, Ovi's audio and video objects.
- **Stage** — find a crowd.

Open it in Chrome on your phone and tap **Install** to put it on your home screen. Works offline after the first visit.
